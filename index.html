  • 1Store the unit prices as variables: bread_price = 2 and juice_price = 2.45.
  • 2Store the quantities: bread_qty = 3 and juice_qty = 2.
  • 3Calcutate bread_total and juice_total as separate variables.
  • 4Calculate subtotal (bread_total + juice_total).
  • 5Calculate discount as 10% of the subtotal and total after discount.
  • 6Print the receipt lines shown in the expected output below. Use str() to convert numbers when building the strings.

Bread x3: 6 EUR
Juice x2: 4.9 EUR
